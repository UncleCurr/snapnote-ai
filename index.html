    How hybrid inference works

    On-device

    Short notes are summarized right on your device with Gemini Nano — private, fast, free, and it works offline.

    In the cloud

    Long documents and complex requests go to Gemini in the cloud, where a larger model handles the heavy lifting.

    Hybrid, automatically

    Firebase AI Logic picks the right place to run each request, so you get a great result whether you are online or off — and you always see where it ran.
